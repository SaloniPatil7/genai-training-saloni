NovaBank fixed deposits are available for periods from 3 months to 5 years.
The minimum fixed deposit amount is ₹5,000.
Customers can choose a tenure in the available tenure range.
Interest is calculated according to the selected fictional FD rate.
Interest rates may differ according to the selected tenure.
Premature withdrawal may result in a reduced interest payout.
Customers can request premature closure through supported channels.
FD maturity proceeds are credited to the linked savings account.
The maturity date is shown when the fixed deposit is created.
All fixed deposit rules in this document are fictional.
