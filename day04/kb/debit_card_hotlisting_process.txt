NovaBank customers can hotlist a lost debit card through the mobile app.
The customer must open the Cards section.
The customer must select the affected debit card.
The customer must choose the Hotlist Card option.
The customer must select a reason for hotlisting.
The customer must confirm the request using the registered mobile number.
A hotlisted card cannot be used for new transactions.
A replacement card can be requested after hotlisting.
The replacement card is delivered within five working days.
The hotlisting reference number is shown after successful submission.
