NovaBank customers should first register a complaint with customer support.
Level 1 complaints are handled by the customer support team.
If unresolved, the complaint can be escalated to Level 2.
Level 2 complaints are reviewed by the service resolution team.
If unresolved after Level 2, the customer can request Level 3 review.
Level 3 complaints are reviewed by the grievance officer.
Customers should keep the complaint reference number during escalation.
Each escalation should include the previous complaint details.
The escalation process applies to supported banking service complaints.
All escalation rules in this document are fictional training data.
