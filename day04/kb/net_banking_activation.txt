NovaBank customers can activate net banking through the official mobile app.
The customer must select the Net Banking option.
The customer must enter the registered mobile number.
An OTP is sent to the registered mobile number.
The customer must verify the OTP to continue activation.
The customer must create a net banking login password.
The password must contain at least eight characters.
Activation is completed after the password is successfully created.
Customers should never share their OTP or password with another person.
