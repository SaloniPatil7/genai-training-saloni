NovaBank customers have a daily UPI transaction limit of ₹50,000.
The maximum amount for one UPI transaction is ₹25,000.
Customers can make up to 20 UPI transactions per day.
UPI limits are reset at midnight based on NovaBank system time.
A failed UPI transaction may remain pending for a short period.
A failed transaction with a debit may be automatically reversed.
Customers should wait up to 24 hours for an automatic reversal.
If the amount is not reversed after 24 hours, support can investigate.
Customers should keep the transaction reference number for support.
