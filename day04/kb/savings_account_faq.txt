NovaBank offers a standard savings account for individual customers.
The minimum opening deposit for the account is ₹1,000.
Customers must maintain a monthly average balance of ₹2,000.
The savings account earns interest at a synthetic rate of 3 percent annually.
Interest is calculated monthly and credited quarterly.
Customers receive a free digital account statement every month.
Mobile banking access is available after account activation.
Customers can update their registered mobile number through a branch.
