NovaTrust Bank - Fixed Deposit Basics

A fixed deposit allows customers to keep money for a selected period.
NovaTrust Bank offers fictional fixed deposit periods from 6 to 60 months.
The minimum fixed deposit amount is 5000 NovaCredits.
Interest depends on the selected deposit period.
Interest rates may vary according to the applicable deposit terms.
Customers can choose maturity instructions when creating the deposit.
Premature withdrawal may be subject to applicable conditions.
The maturity amount includes the principal and applicable interest.
Fixed deposits are separate from regular savings account balances.
Customers should review the deposit terms before confirming the deposit.
