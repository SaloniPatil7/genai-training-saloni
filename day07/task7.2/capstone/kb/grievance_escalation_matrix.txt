NovaTrust Bank - Grievance Escalation Matrix

Level 1 is the normal customer support channel.
Customers should first report an issue through the normal support channel.
The support team should record the customer's complaint reference.

Level 2 is the senior support team.
Issues that cannot be resolved by normal support should be escalated.
Repeated unresolved complaints should be reviewed by the senior support team.

Level 3 is urgent escalation.
Urgent unauthorized transaction concerns should be escalated immediately.
Transaction disputes should include the relevant transaction reference.

Card-related complaints should include the last four card digits when available.
Customers should keep their complaint reference for follow-up.
The escalation team reviews the available complaint information.
A human support agent may request additional information when required.
