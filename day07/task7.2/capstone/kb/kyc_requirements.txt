NovaTrust Bank - KYC Requirements

KYC is required before customers can access certain banking services.
Customers must provide an approved identity document.
Customers must also provide required contact information.
Additional verification may be required for some services.
KYC information must match the customer's submitted records.
Customers may be asked to complete verification again when required.
Incomplete KYC may restrict access to selected services.
Customers should submit documents only through approved NovaTrust channels.
KYC updates are subject to identity verification.
Customers should not share confidential credentials with anyone.
