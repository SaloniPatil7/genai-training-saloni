NovaTrust Bank - Savings Account FAQ

NovaTrust Bank offers a basic savings account for personal banking.
The minimum opening deposit is 500 NovaCredits.
The minimum balance requirement is 1000 NovaCredits.
Customers can access their account through mobile banking and net banking.
Savings accounts support domestic fund transfers.
Interest is calculated monthly and credited quarterly.
Customers can request an account statement through net banking.
A customer can update contact details after identity verification.
The savings account does not support international cash withdrawals.
For account problems, customers can contact NovaTrust support.
