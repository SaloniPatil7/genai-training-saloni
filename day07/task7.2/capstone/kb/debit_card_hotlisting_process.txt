NovaTrust Bank - Debit Card Hotlisting Process

Customers should hotlist a lost or stolen debit card immediately.
Hotlisting permanently blocks the card from further transactions.
Customers can request hotlisting through NovaTrust mobile banking.
Customers can also request hotlisting through net banking.
The last four digits of the card may be required for identification.
After successful hotlisting, a reference number is generated.
A hotlisted card cannot be reactivated.
Customers who need a replacement card can submit a replacement request.
Replacement processing is separate from card hotlisting.
Customers should report suspicious transactions immediately.
