NovaTrust Bank - Credit Card Types

NovaTrust Bank offers three fictional credit cards.
The Nova Classic card is designed for everyday purchases.
The Nova Rewards card provides reward points on eligible purchases.
The Nova Travel card is designed for travel-related spending.
The Classic card has a fictional annual fee of 500 NovaCredits.
The Rewards card has a fictional annual fee of 1000 NovaCredits.
The Travel card has a fictional annual fee of 1500 NovaCredits.
Annual fees are charged once every membership year.
Eligibility depends on the customer's profile and verification.
Customers should check the approved card terms before applying.
