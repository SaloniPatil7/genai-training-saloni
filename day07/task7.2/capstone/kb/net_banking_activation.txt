NovaTrust Bank - Net Banking Activation

Customers can activate net banking through the NovaTrust banking portal.
The customer must have an active NovaTrust account.
Identity verification is required during activation.
The customer creates a login ID during the activation process.
A secure authentication method is required to complete activation.
Customers must create a strong password.
Login credentials should never be shared with another person.
Customers can reset their password through the approved recovery process.
Repeated failed login attempts may temporarily restrict access.
Customers should contact NovaTrust support if activation fails repeatedly.
